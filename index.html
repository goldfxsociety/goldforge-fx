What is Forex?

Forex (Foreign Exchange) is the global market where currencies are traded. With over $7.5 trillion in daily volume, it's the largest financial market in the world — open 24 hours a day, 5 days a week.

Why Trade Gold?

Gold (XAUUSD) is one of the most traded instruments in Forex. It has high liquidity, clear trends, and responds predictably to global news — making it ideal for both beginners and experienced traders.

How Trading Works

Traders profit by buying low and selling high (or selling high and buying back lower). You use a broker platform to open trades, set stop-losses to manage risk, and take profits at your target price.
